# Data retention proposal — NOT APPROVED / NO AUTOMATIC DELETION

Reviewed 26 October 2026. This proposal is operational guidance for operator and Nigerian privacy-law review, not a statement that these periods are legally required or currently enforced. No cron job, queue consumer, automatic purge or privileged erasure executor is activated by this change.

## Research and decisions needed

Primary references: [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, NDPC](https://www.ndpc.gov.ng/ndp-act-2023/), sections 24–27 (purpose, lawful grounds and transparency), 31 (children), 34–38 (rights), 39–43 (security/transfers), and [NDPC GAID 2025](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf). Official search-index excerpts were accessible; direct retrieval of those official pages/PDF returned HTTP 403 in this environment. Recheck the full current Act/GAID with Nigerian counsel before relying on article-specific procedures or deadlines. Do not treat older GAID drafts as final law.

The core legal requirements are purpose-limited storage, an appropriate lawful processing basis, security, transparency and handling erasure/restriction rights without undue delay, subject to specific justified grounds. There is no general legal requirement identified here that every marketplace record must be retained for a fixed number of years. The numbers below are recommendations, not statutory periods. Litigation, statutory, regulatory or safety holds require a recorded scope, decision-maker, authority, expiry/review date and restricted access; a report is not an automatic indefinite hold. Propose review every 90 days while a hold exists. Counsel must determine actual applicable limitation periods and exceptions.

Student status is not proof of adult capacity. Section 31 consent/guardian and age requirements need a specific assessment for 16–17-year-olds; do not assume that a commercial marketplace qualifies for an education exception. The service currently has no completed guardian-consent/age-verification workflow and must not claim otherwise. Confirm lawful grounds/capacity and minimise public contact/location exposure before launch. No new birthdate/document collection is introduced here.

## Required approval before enforcement

1. Name the accountable legal operator and privacy contact; confirm any NDPC registration/DPO requirements without claiming an unverified status.
2. Approve purposes/lawful grounds and assessments for verification, raw analytics, public contact data, and minors; review guardian/age arrangements.
3. Confirm provider agreements, regions, cross-border mechanisms and actual Auth/log/backup retention. Verify both database and Storage restore.
4. Approve each duration and hold/appeal procedure, rare-event aggregation rules and access to restricted evidence. Train reviewers to avoid adding new sensitive data to messages.
5. Build and test the separately authorised erasure executor and deleted-token denial before offering permanent completion. Review/reconcile its deletion journal against backups and storage.
6. Publish approved notices; only then approve any cleanup schedules with dry runs, bounded batches, monitoring and rollback/recovery drills. Nothing in this PR activates automatic deletion.
